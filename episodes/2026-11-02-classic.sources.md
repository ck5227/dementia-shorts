- TCTMD（2019）：https://www.tctmd.com/news/intensive-bp-lowering-may-have-cognitive-benefits-sprint-mind
  - 9,361 hypertensive, nondiabetic；mean age 68；35.6% women
  - 目標 <120 vs <140 mm Hg；median treatment 3.34 y；median follow-up 5.11 y

  - MCI or probable dementia 20.2 vs 24.1，HR 0.85 (0.74–0.97)
  - 提早終止、失智事件數少
