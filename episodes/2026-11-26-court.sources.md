## 主要證據 1：GEM 試驗（美國，JAMA 2008）
- DeKosky ST, et al. Ginkgo biloba for prevention of dementia: a randomized controlled trial. JAMA. 2008 Nov 19. PMCID PMC2823569
- 摘要（SFDA 逐字轉載）：https://www.sfda.gov.sa/en/awarenessarticle/ginkgo-biloba-prevention-dementia
  - "Three thousand sixty-nine community volunteers aged 75 years or older with normal cognition (n = 2587) or MCI (n = 482)" → 畫面「美國 3 千位 75 歲以上」
  - 120 mg twice daily；median follow-up 6.1 years → 「每天吃」「追蹤約 6 年」
  - "Five hundred twenty-three individuals developed dementia (246 receiving placebo and 277 receiving G biloba)"
  - "HR ... all-cause dementia was 1.12 (95% CI, 0.94-1.33; P = .21) and for AD, 1.16 (95% CI, 0.97-1.39; P = .11)"
  - MCI 進展：HR 1.13（0.85–1.50；P=.39）
  - 結論：not effective in reducing either the overall incidence rate of dementia or AD incidence
- Table 2：https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2823569/table/T2
  - Placebo n=1524，G biloba n=1545；all dementia 2.94/100 PY (246) vs 3.27 (277)，HR 1.12 (0.94–1.33)
  - 換算：277/1545 = 17.9% → 每 100 人 18 人；246/1524 = 16.1% → 每 100 人 16 人 → bars 18 vs 16
  - 血管性失智（無 AD）17 vs 7，HR 0.41（0.17–0.98，P=.05）——次要、事件極少，只放說明欄
- NEJM Journal Watch：https://clinician.nejm.org/JP200811180000001
  - "Dementia was diagnosed in 523 participants (placebo, 16.1%; ginkgo, 17.9%)"
  - "the number of hemorrhagic strokes was nonsignificantly higher in the gingko group"（無人數）
  - 設計可偵測失智少 30%，檢定力 96%
- NIH 新聞稿（2008-11-24）：https://www.nih.gov/node/8756
  - "The products used in the study were supplied by Schwabe Pharmaceuticals, a German company."
  - "GEM, co-funded by 5 NIH components and other sources"
  - "there also appeared to be no significant side effects from ginkgo"
- 匹茲堡大學新聞：https://www.chronicle.pitt.edu/story/ginkgo-biloba-does-not-cut-dementia-risk-pitt-led-study-finds
  - 經費：NCCAM、NIA，NHLBI 支援；"Mortality rates also were similar"；不影響冠心病或中風發生率
- GEM 認知功能追蹤：Snitz BE, et al. JAMA. 2009;302(24):2663-2670. DOI 10.1001/jama.2009.1913 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2832285
  - 3069 人，72–96 歲，median 6.1 年；"Annual rates of decline in z scores did not differ between G biloba and placebo groups in any domains, including memory"

## 主要證據 2：GuidAge 試驗（法國，Lancet Neurol 2012）
- Vellas B, et al. Long-term use of standardised ginkgo biloba extract for the prevention of Alzheimer's disease (GuidAge). Lancet Neurol. 2012;11(10):851-859. DOI 10.1016/S1474-4422(12)70206-5 · NCT00276510
- 摘要（CORE）：https://core.ac.uk/download/52411637.pdf
  - ≥70 歲、主動向家醫科醫師抱怨記性差（法國）；2854 人隨機；EGb761 120 mg 每天兩次 vs 假藥；追蹤 5 年 → 畫面「法國約 2 千 8 百人、吃 5 年」
  - 1406 vs 1414 人（至少服一劑）；AD 61 vs 73（1.2 vs 1.4 /100 人年）；HR 0.84（95% CI 0.60–1.18；p=0.306）；風險不符比例假設
  - 死亡 76 vs 82（0.94，0.69–1.28）；中風 65 vs 60（RR 1.12，0.77–1.63）；出血或心血管事件無差異
  - 資金來源：em-consulte 摘要 "Funding: Ipsen." https://www.em-consulte.com/article/752956（查核員確認）
- 交叉核對：Alzforum https://alzforum.org/node/36868；medwireNews https://www.news-medical.net/news/20120906/Hopes-dashed-for-ginkgo-biloba-in-Alzheimers-disease.aspx（HR 0.84，61/1406 vs 73/1414）

## 最新系統性回顧：Cochrane 2026
- Wieland LS, et al. Ginkgo biloba for cognitive impairment and dementia. Cochrane Database Syst Rev. 2026, Issue 2, CD013661.pub2. DOI 10.1002/14651858.CD013661.pub2（2026-02-05，搜尋至 2024-11）
  - https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD013661_what-are-benefits-and-risks-ginkgo-biloba-cognitive-impairment-and-dementia
  - 82 個研究、10,613 人；對象是主觀記性差、MS、MCI、失智者（不是預防研究）
  - MCI：12 研究 1913 人，6 個月 "probably not better than placebo"（中等確定性）
  - 已失智：13 研究 3288 人，6 個月 "may be small to moderate benefits"（低確定性，I² 88–96%，多數研究在中國，少有超過 6 個月）→ caveat「已經失智的人，還不確定」
  - 不良事件：MCI／失智 "probably no increased risk of harm"（12 個月內）

## 安全性：美國 NIH NCCIH
- https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/providers/digest/herb-drug-interactions-science
  - "Taking Ginkgo biloba with warfarin is associated with increased risk for major bleeding events compared to warfarin alone."
  - "Ginkgo has not been shown to be beneficial for preventing or slowing the progression of dementia."
- https://nccih.nih.gov/health/ginkgo/ataglance.htm（2025-02 更新）
  - "Ginkgo may increase the risk of bleeding in people who are taking anticoagulant drugs, such as warfarin." → caveat「吃抗凝血藥，可能易出血」
  - "Fresh ginkgo seeds are toxic when consumed orally"；roasted seeds 也有嚴重副作用案例 → caveat「生的銀杏果有毒」
  - 常見副作用：頭暈、腸胃不適、頭痛
  - "In three large studies in older adults with normal cognitive function or mild cognitive impairment, supplementation with ginkgo extract did not reduce the risk of dementia."

## takeaway 替代做法（本頻道先前已查核的研究）
